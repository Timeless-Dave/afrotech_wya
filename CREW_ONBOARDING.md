Hey — this is our **Where Are You** board for AfroTech. Same link for everyone.

**Join (30 seconds)**  
1. Open the link → tap **Join**  
2. Type **your name** (use a last initial if someone else has the same first name)  
3. Enter the **group code** we shared  
4. Tap **Add to Home Screen** (or the install banner) so it opens like an app  
5. Turn on **check-in alerts** on the **Me** tab  

**Stay findable**  
- **Me** or **Map:** pick your floor + room → **Still here** when you move  
- **Live location** only while you’re walking between halls (stops when you close the app)  
- **Map → Meet me here** so people know exactly where to meet you  
- **Friends** refreshes every ~12s while that tab is open  
- **Crew:** pair with someone to see **Call / Text** after they save their number  

**Guide** works offline after the first load (agenda, map, free food). Check-ins need cell data or Wi‑Fi.

Log out on **Me** if you hand your phone to someone else.
